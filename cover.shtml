Welcome to my Web Site



    Dr. Livescu is a Fellow of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME), an Associate Fellow of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA), and the recipient of the 2017 Frank Harlow Distinguished Mentor Award. He is a member of the Editorial Committee of the Annual Review of Fluid Mechanics, an Associate Editor for AIAA Journal, and a member of the AIAA CFD Vision 2030 Integration and Outreach Committee. Dr. Livescu was part of the Executive Committee of the APS Division of Computational Physics (2020-2023) and a member of the AIAA Fluid Dynamics Technical Committee (2020-2023) and is also a past Associate Editor for ASME Journal of Fluids Engineering. He has been part of the organizing committee for the International Symposium on Combustion ( 2022 , 2024 ), as Turbulent Flames Colloquium Co-Chair.
